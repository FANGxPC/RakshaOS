## Slide 3: The Gap
**Existing tools (Truecaller, Antivirus) only filter *known* bad numbers or links.**
They ask: *"Who is the sender?"*

**The real question ordinary people need answered is:**
*"Is what I am being asked to DO right now actually safe?"*

---

## Slide 4: Our Solution (RakshaOS)
A digital safety layer that sits between the user and a suspicious message.
- **Scam Genome:** Identifies behavioral signatures (artificial urgency + authority impersonation + payment request), not just keywords.
- **The "Why" Engine:** Explains *why* something is dangerous in plain, simple language that a grandparent can understand.
- **Full Lifecycle:** Prevent → Detect → Explain → Respond → Recover.

## Slide 6: Architecture (Technical Implementation)
**4-Layer System:**
1. **Perception:** OCR & QR decoding (Tesseract, pyzbar)
2. **Reasoning:** Scam Genome Library + LLM (Gemini 2.5 Flash / Gemma 4)
3. **Decision:** Risk Engine (Heuristics + AI)
4. **Response:** Plain-language generation & Action recommendations
